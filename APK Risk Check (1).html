What it could cost you

Expected loss is the risk percentage multiplied by the money you could lose if the app is fraudulent. Enter the money reachable from the phone: bank balance, UPI limit or card limit.

Cost-benefit of screening apps

For an app store, bank or college lab choosing how to screen APKs. It compares three setups on avoided loss, running cost, false-alarm cost, present worth and return on investment. All figures are example assumptions that you can change.

The three setups

Detection and false-alarm rates for static screening come from this project’s test run (88.7% and 1.3%). The other two rows are example assumptions.

The link score looks at the address itself: where it points, how it is written, and whether it imitates a known brand. A link cannot show what is inside an APK, so a low score does not prove an app is safe. The money risks list the usual outcomes of the patterns found, not a guarantee of loss. The expected-loss and cost-benefit figures are planning aids built on assumptions you can edit. Treat HIGH and CRITICAL as a reason not to install. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is downloaded or sent anywhere.
